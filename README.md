## 🎨 Visual Identity & Subtle Dark Architecture

* **Theme:** Refined Subtle Dark Theme (`#0A0D12`, `#111620`, `#171E2C`) with high-contrast text (`#FFFFFF`, `#E2E8F0`, `#94A3B8`).
* **Accents:** Luminous Royal Gold & Warm Amber (`#F59E0B`, `#FBBF24`), Radiant Emerald Green (`#10B981`), and Heritage Bharatgas Blue (`#3B82F6`).
* **Glassmorphism:** Frosted translucent cards (`rgba(17, 23, 34, 0.78)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(20px)`), subtle gold borders, and ambient glow shadows.
* **Typography:** Elegant Serif (`Playfair Display`, `Cinzel`) with crisp modern Sans-Serif (`Plus Jakarta Sans`).
* **Framer Motion Animations:** Smooth scroll reveals, staggered card animations, interactive hover elevations, and physics smooth momentum scrolling.
